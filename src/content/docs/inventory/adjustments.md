Use **Inventory → Adjust / write off** to record a correction or a loss with a reason. Every
entry is added to the ledger and to the audit log.

## Two kinds

- **Write-off (stock leaves)** — a loss: damaged, expired, stolen, used in the shop. Enter how many
  are lost; they are taken out of stock.
- **Adjustment (up or down)** — a correction. Enter the change: `3` adds three, `-2` takes two away.

## Post an adjustment or write-off

1. **Choose a store**.
2. **Choose a reason**. The reason decides whether it is a write-off or an adjustment.
3. **Add an item**: search name or SKU, then enter the quantity. Add as many items as you need.
4. Add a **Note (optional)**.
5. Select <span class="ui">Post write-off</span> or <span class="ui">Post adjustment</span>.

For a full recount of a shelf or store, use a [stock count](/inventory/counts/) instead.

## Stock adjustment reasons

Manage reasons in **Settings → Stock adjustment reasons**. Ready-made:

| Reason | Kind |
|--------|------|
| Count correction | Adjustment |
| Found | Adjustment |
| Damaged | Write-off |
| Expired | Write-off |
| Theft / missing | Write-off |
| Own use | Write-off |

Select <span class="ui">Add stock reason</span> to add your own: enter the name, choose the
**Kind** and save. Switch off reasons you do not use.

The **Adjustments & write-offs** report shows what was adjusted or written off, by store and
reason, with its value.
