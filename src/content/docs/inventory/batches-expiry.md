For food, medicine and cosmetics you can track **batches** (lots) with **expiry dates**. This
feature depends on your plan.

## Turn it on for a product

On the product, tick **Track batches and expiry dates (asked when receiving)**. When you
[receive goods](/purchasing/receiving-goods/) for it, you enter the **Batch no.** and
**Expiry** date.

## How stock is taken

Sales, transfers and write-offs take from the batch that **expires first**.

## The Expiry tab

**Inventory → Expiry** shows what is left of each received batch, earliest expiry first.

1. Choose the **Store**.
2. Choose **Expiring** — *within … days (and expired)*, or *All batches*.
3. Each row shows the item, **Batch no.**, **Expiry**, *… days left* or **Expired**, and the
   quantity. The total **Value of these batches** is shown.

To remove expired or damaged stock, select <span class="ui">Write off</span> on the batch, enter
the quantity and confirm.

Stock received before batches were tracked is not listed.
