## GPOS addresses

| | Address | Who uses it |
|-|---------|-------------|
| **Back office** | **[https://app.gpos.lk](https://app.gpos.lk)** | Owners, managers, inventory staff, accountants |
| **Till / POS** | **[https://app.gpos.lk/pos/](https://app.gpos.lk/pos/)** | Cashiers, on the counter computer or tablet |

<Aside type="caution" title="Features depend on your plan">
GPOS comes in different plans — for example **Basic**, **Standard** or **Custom**. Some features in
this guide (such as inventory, purchasing, customers, several branches or expiry tracking) may not
be in your plan. Check the **Plan** card on
your dashboard and see [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).
</Aside>

## New to GPOS?

Not registered yet? **[Apply for GPOS](/getting-started/apply/)** online at
[app.gpos.lk/register](https://app.gpos.lk/register).

If your business has just been enrolled, start with the **[Quick start](/quick-start/)**. It walks
you through the eight steps of the setup checklist you will see on your dashboard, and gets you to
your first sale in about an hour.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Back office" icon="laptop">
    The web admin where owners and managers set up branches, staff, products, prices, taxes and
    payment methods, and follow sales, stock and cash.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Till (POS)" icon="rocket">
    The checkout screen on your counter computer or tablet. It scans barcodes, takes cash, card
    and QR payments, and **keeps selling when the internet is down**.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Inventory & purchasing" icon="list-format">
    Opening stock, adjustments, stock counts, transfers between stores, purchase orders, goods
    receiving and expiry dates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reports" icon="document">
    Daily sales, best sellers, payments, tax summary, cash over/short, stock value and more —
    export to CSV or print to PDF.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
