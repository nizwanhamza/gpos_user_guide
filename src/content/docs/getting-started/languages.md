GPOS — the back office, the till and this guide — is available in **English**, **සිංහල (Sinhala)**
and **தமிழ் (Tamil)**.

## Which language is used?

1. **Your own choice** — the language switch on the sign-in page and in the app.
2. **Your employee setting** — an administrator can set each employee's **Language** in
   **Employees → Edit**. *Business default* follows the business.
3. **The business default** — set by the owner in **Settings → Regional settings → Default language**.

## Sinhala and Tamil product names

Customers like to read receipts in their own language. To add Sinhala and Tamil names:

1. Go to **Settings → Products and prices** and tick **Use Sinhala/Tamil product names**.
2. Select <span class="ui">Save settings</span>.
3. Each product now has optional **Sinhala name** and **Tamil name** fields. In the import
   spreadsheet use the `name_si` and `name_ta` columns.

The till and receipts use these names when they are filled in. Payment methods, cash in/out
reasons and similar lists also have optional Sinhala and Tamil names.

## This guide

Use the language menu at the top of any page to switch. Every page is available in all three
languages. The Sinhala and Tamil pages use everyday language and keep technical words — and the
names of buttons and menus — in English, as they appear in the English app.
