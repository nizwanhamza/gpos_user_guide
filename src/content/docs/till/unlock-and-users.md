Cashiers do not type passwords on the till. They choose their name and enter their **6-digit POS
PIN**. This also works **offline**.

## Unlock

1. On **Who is using the till?**, select your name.
2. **Enter your PIN** on the number pad (or keyboard).

Not on the list? The list shows people who **have a POS PIN** and **may sell at this branch**.
Ask an administrator to set your PIN in **Employees**, or set it yourself in
[My account](/getting-started/my-account/). The till receives it on its next sync.

If the list says *No cashiers yet*, nobody has a PIN for this branch yet.

## Wrong PIN

After **5 wrong PINs** the till locks that person out for 5 minutes, and the wait gets longer each
time. The till shows how long to wait.

## Lock and change user

- Select <span class="ui">Lock</span> when you leave the counter. The till asks for a PIN again.
- Select <span class="ui">Change user</span> to hand over to a colleague — the till goes back to
  *Who is using the till?*. A colleague can also choose <span class="ui">Someone else</span> on the
  lock screen.
- The till **locks itself after 10 minutes** without use.

:::tip
Everything done on the till — sales, discounts, refunds, cash out, approvals — is recorded under
the person who unlocked it. Always lock the till when you step away.
:::

## What you may do

The till shows what your role allows. For example: if you may not sell, you see *You can look up
products, but not sell on this till.* Discounts above your limit, returns and voids may need a
[manager's approval](/till/discounts/#manager-approval).
