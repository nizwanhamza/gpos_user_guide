:::note[Depends on your plan]
This page is part of the **POS checkout, cash shifts and POS devices** feature. If it is not in your plan, you will not see it in the menu. See [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).
:::

Before your first sale of the day, a **shift must be open** on the till — see
[Cash shifts on the till](/till/shifts/).

## 1. Add items

Any of these adds an item to the **Sale** on the right:

- **Scan a barcode.** You do not need to click anywhere first — the scan box picks it up. A pack
  barcode adds the whole pack (for example *Pack of 24*).
- **Type** a name or SKU in **Scan a barcode or type a name or SKU**, then pick the item — with
  the mouse, or with **↑ ↓** and **Enter**.
- **Tap a quick-pick button** for items without barcodes.

Barcode not found? If your plan includes it, select <span class="ui">Add as a new product</span>
and sell it straight away — see [Add a product at the till](/till/add-product-at-till/).

Each result shows the price (*incl. tax* or *plus tax*), the tax group and, if stock is tracked,
*In stock here: …*.

### Items sold by weight

For products sold by weight the till asks for **Weight on the scale (kg)**. Type the weight, for
example `1.250`, and select <span class="ui">Add to sale</span>.

## 2. Check the sale

For each line you can use <span class="ui">One more</span> / <span class="ui">One less</span>, or
select <span class="ui">Edit</span> on the line (**F1** edits the last item added) to change:

- the **Quantity**;
- the **Variant** — another size or colour of the same product;
- the **Discount** on this line — see [Discounts](/till/discounts/);
- or <span class="ui">Remove item</span>.

Select <span class="ui">Save</span>. On a keyboard, **Num +** and **Num −** add or take away one of
the last item.

The bottom shows the **Subtotal**, **Tax** (or *Tax included*) and **Total**.

Need to start again? <span class="ui">Clear</span> (or **Ctrl + Delete**) removes all lines (you
are asked to confirm).

## 3. Add a customer (optional)

Select <span class="ui">Customer</span> (**F3**) to attach a customer and apply their discount
automatically. If you have not added one, the till asks **Customer for this sale?** when you pay:
<span class="ui">Add customer</span> (**F3** or **Enter**), or <span class="ui">Skip — pay now</span>
(**F4** again). See [Customers at the till](/till/customers/).

## 4. Take payment

1. Select <span class="ui">Pay</span> or press **F4**.
2. Choose the **Payment method**. For **cash**, enter **Cash received** (or select
   <span class="ui">Exact</span>); the till shows the **Change**.
3. Select <span class="ui">Complete sale</span> (or press **F4** / **Enter**).

Card, QR, bank transfer and split payments are explained in [Payments](/till/payments/).

## 5. Change and receipt

The till shows **Sale complete · Receipt …** with the **Change** to give in large figures, and
asks **Print a receipt for the customer?**

- <span class="ui">Print receipt (80 mm)</span> — **Enter** or **F5**; or
- <span class="ui">Go Green — no receipt</span> — **Esc**. No paper is used.

If the till is set to **Print after each sale** ([Till settings](/till/shortcuts-and-settings/#receipt-printing)),
it prints at once without asking.

Customer wants the receipt after all? Press **F5** to print the last receipt again. Older sales:
**F9** (*Sales*) → the sale → reprint, or <span class="ui">Print A4</span> for a full-page
**invoice**. Reprints are marked **COPY**.

## Works offline

All of this also works **without internet**. The sale is saved on the till and sent to the back
office as soon as the connection is back. See [Offline and sync](/till/offline-sync/).

:::note[Prices may be out of date]
If the till has been offline a long time it shows *Prices may be out of date: last update …*.
Connect it to the internet so it can sync.
:::
