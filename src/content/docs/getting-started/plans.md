:::caution[Features depend on your plan]
GPOS is offered in different **plans** — for example **Basic**, **Standard** or a **Custom** plan
agreed for your business. **Not every feature in this guide is included in every plan.** If you
cannot find something described here, it is probably not part of your plan.
:::

## What a plan decides

### Features that can be switched on or off

| Feature | What it covers | Guide pages |
|---------|----------------|-------------|
| **POS checkout, cash shifts and POS devices** | The till, sales, payment methods, discounts, returns and voids, cash shifts, POS devices | [Using the till](/till/making-a-sale/), [Sales](/sales/sales-history/), [Cash shifts](/sales/cash-shifts/), [POS devices](/business/pos-devices/) |
| **Stock ledger, counts, adjustments and transfers** | Inventory: stock levels, opening stock, adjustments, stock counts, transfers | [Inventory](/inventory/stock-and-movements/) |
| **Suppliers, purchase orders and goods receiving** | Purchasing | [Purchasing](/purchasing/suppliers/) |
| **Customer profiles and customer discounts** | Customers in the back office and at the till, including PDPA export and erase | [Customers](/sales/customers/) |
| **More than one active branch** | Running several shops | [Branches](/business/branches/) |
| **Batch/lot and expiry tracking** | Batch numbers and expiry dates | [Batches and expiry](/inventory/batches-expiry/) |
| **Add new products at the till** | Adding an unknown barcode as a product from the till | [Add a product at the till](/till/add-product-at-till/) |

Pages about these features carry a **Depends on your plan** note.

### Limits

A plan can also set **how many** you may have of:

- **Active branches**
- **Active registers** (checkout counters, one till each)
- **Active employees**
- **Products** (archived products do not count)

### Always included

Signing in (including two-step sign-in), the dashboard, business settings, employees and roles, products and the catalog
(categories, brands, units, taxes, import and export), reports for the features you have, and the
audit log.

## See your plan

Open the **Dashboard**. The **Plan** card shows:

- the **name** of your plan (*Plan: Standard*, for example);
- **every feature** with **✓** (included) or **✕** (not included);
- each **limit** — how many you use and how many are allowed (or *unlimited*), and *limit reached*
  when you are at the maximum.

## When a feature is not in your plan

- Its **menu items do not appear** — for example, without the inventory feature there is no
  **Inventory** in the menu.
- Related **setup checklist steps are left out** — for example *Pair the first till* when the plan
  has no POS.
- If you try anyway, GPOS shows **"This feature is not included in your plan"**.
- At a limit — for example adding one more branch or employee — GPOS shows **"Your plan limit has
  been reached"**. Deactivating a branch or disabling an employee frees up a place.

## Change your plan

*To change your plan, contact your service provider.* Once your plan is changed, the new features
appear the next time you open or reload the back office — nothing needs to be reinstalled. Data
you entered before stays as it is.
