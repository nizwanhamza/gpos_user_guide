:::note[Depends on your plan]
Your plan can limit the number of **active employees**. Disabled employees do not count. See
[Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).
:::

Everyone who uses GPOS — in the back office or at the till — has their own **employee** account.
Never share accounts: GPOS records who did what.

## Add an employee

1. Go to **Employees** → <span class="ui">Add employee</span>.
2. Enter the **Full name**, **Email** and an **Initial password** (at least 12 characters).
3. Choose a **Role** and where it applies:
   - **Branch** — the branch they work in, or *All branches*.
   - **Register** — usually *Whole branch*; pick a register to limit them to one counter.
4. Need more? Select <span class="ui">+ Add another role</span> — for example *Cashier* in Kandy and
   *Manager* in Colombo.
5. Select <span class="ui">Create employee</span>.

Give the employee their business code, e-mail and initial password. At their first sign-in they
choose their own password. The number of employees you can have depends on your plan.

## Set a POS PIN for an employee

Cashiers unlock the till with a **6-digit PIN**. To set it for them:

1. Select the employee to open **Manage {name}**.
2. Select <span class="ui">Set POS PIN</span>, enter the PIN twice and save.
3. Tell them the PIN **in person**.

Tills receive the PIN on their next sync. Employees can also set or change their own PIN in
[My account](/getting-started/my-account/). Only people with a POS PIN who may sell at a branch
appear on that branch's till.

## Change roles

Open the employee and change the roles, branches or registers, then select
<span class="ui">Save roles</span>. Changes apply on the employee's next action — they do not need
to sign out.

## Edit details and language

Select <span class="ui">Edit</span> to change the name, e-mail or **Language** (*Business default*,
English, Sinhala or Tamil).

## Reset a password

If someone forgets their password and cannot use the e-mail link:

1. Open the employee and select <span class="ui">Reset password</span>.
2. GPOS shows a **temporary password — only once**. Give it to the employee.
3. They are signed out everywhere and must choose a new password at their next sign-in.

## Reset two-step sign-in

If an employee **lost their phone** (and their recovery codes), open the employee and select
<span class="ui">Reset two-step sign-in</span>, then confirm. Their two-step sign-in is turned off
(*Two-step sign-in was turned off*). They sign in with their password and set it up again on the
new phone — straight away if your business requires it.

Do this only after you have checked it is really them asking. You need the *Create, update and
disable employees and their roles* permission.

## Disable or enable

When someone leaves, select <span class="ui">Disable</span>. They are **signed out immediately**
and can no longer use the back office or the tills. Their history stays. Select
<span class="ui">Enable</span> to bring the account back.

:::tip
The *Active employees* count on the dashboard shows how close you are to your plan's limit.
Disabled employees do not count.
:::
