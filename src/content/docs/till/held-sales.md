:::note[Depends on your plan]
This page is part of the **POS checkout, cash shifts and POS devices** feature. If it is not in your plan, you will not see it in the menu. See [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).
:::

A customer forgot something? **Hold** their sale and serve the next person.

1. Select <span class="ui">Hold (F6)</span>. The sale is put aside and the screen is cleared.
2. Serve other customers as usual.
3. Select <span class="ui">Held (n)</span> (or press **F7**) to see **Held sales**.
4. Select <span class="ui">Resume</span> to bring the sale back — or <span class="ui">Discard</span>
   to throw it away.

If you resume while another sale is in progress, that one is held in its place.

:::caution
Held sales stay on **this till** only, and are **discarded when the shift closes**. The till
warns you before closing — resume and finish them first if needed.
:::
