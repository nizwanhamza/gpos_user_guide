Select <span class="ui">My account</span> at the bottom of the menu to manage your own sign-in
details: your password, **two-step sign-in** and your POS PIN.

## Change your password

1. Enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter a **New password** (at least 12 characters) and repeat it in **Confirm new password**.
3. Select <span class="ui">Change password</span>.

All your other sessions are signed out — for example, if you were still signed in on another
computer.

## Two-step sign-in

Turn on a code from an authenticator app for extra protection, see how many recovery codes are
left, or turn it off. Step by step: [Two-step sign-in](/getting-started/two-step-sign-in/).

## Set or change your POS PIN

The **POS PIN** is a 6-digit number that unlocks the tills of your branch — also when the till is
offline. It **cannot** be used to sign in to the back office.

1. Select <span class="ui">Set POS PIN</span> (or <span class="ui">Change POS PIN</span>).
2. Enter your current **password** to confirm it is you.
3. Enter a **New PIN** of 6 digits and **Repeat the PIN**.
4. Save. Tills receive the new PIN on their **next sync** (usually within a minute when online).

PIN rules:

- exactly **6 digits**;
- not one digit repeated (`111111`);
- not a run (`123456` or `654321`).

:::caution[Keep your PIN to yourself]
Everything done on a till is recorded under the person whose PIN unlocked it — sales, discounts,
refunds, cash taken out and approvals.
:::
