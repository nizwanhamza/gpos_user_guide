The **back office** is the web admin of your business. Owners, managers, inventory staff and
accountants use it from any computer with a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).

:::note[Back office or till?]
Cashiers usually do **not** need to sign in to the back office. They unlock the till with a
6-digit **POS PIN** instead — see [Unlocking the till](/till/unlock-and-users/).
:::

## Sign in

1. Open **[app.gpos.lk](https://app.gpos.lk)** in your browser.
2. Fill in:
   - **Business code** — the short code of your business, for example `kandy-mart`. It is shown on
     the dashboard and under your business name in the menu. Every employee of the business uses
     the same code.
   - **Email** — your own e-mail address.
   - **Password** — your own password.
3. Select <span class="ui">Sign in</span>.
4. If **two-step sign-in** is on for you, enter the 6-digit **Code** from your authenticator app
   (or a recovery code) and select <span class="ui">Continue</span>. See
   [Two-step sign-in](/getting-started/two-step-sign-in/).

If you see **Incorrect business code, email or password**, check all three — the business code is
the most common mistake.

## Your first sign-in

When an administrator creates your account (or resets your password) you get a **temporary
password**. At the first sign-in GPOS asks you to **set a new password**:

1. Enter the **Temporary password**.
2. Enter a **New password** — at least **12 characters**. A memorable phrase, such as
   `blue-tea-at-four-pm`, works well.
3. Repeat it in **Confirm new password** and select <span class="ui">Change password</span>.

You cannot use the back office until you do this. If you prefer, select
<span class="ui">Sign out instead</span>.

## Screens you may see after signing in

### Terms of service (owners)

The **owner** is asked to accept the **Terms of service**, **Privacy policy** and **Data
processing terms** at the first sign-in, and again whenever a new version is published.

1. Open and read each document (the links show the version).
2. Tick **I have read and accept these documents on behalf of the business**.
3. Select <span class="ui">Accept and continue</span>.

Until the owner accepts, the owner cannot use the back office (*Accept the terms first*). Other
employees are not asked and can keep working, and the tills keep selling.

### Set up two-step sign-in

If two-step sign-in is **required** for your account and you have not set it up yet, GPOS shows
**Set up two-step sign-in** before anything else. Follow the steps on the screen — see
[Two-step sign-in](/getting-started/two-step-sign-in/#set-it-up). You can also
<span class="ui">Sign out</span> and do it later, but you cannot use the back office until it is
done.

## Forgot your password?

1. On the sign-in page select <span class="ui">Forgot password?</span>.
2. Enter your **Business code** and **Email**, then select <span class="ui">Send link</span>.
3. Open the e-mail and follow the link (it is valid for **30 minutes** and works once). Check your
   spam folder if it does not arrive.
4. Choose a new password and select <span class="ui">Save new password</span>.

You are then signed out everywhere and sign in again with the new password.

:::note
Resetting your password does not turn off two-step sign-in. Lost your phone as well? See
[Lost or replaced phone](/getting-started/two-step-sign-in/#lost-or-replaced-phone).
:::

:::tip[No e-mail access?]
Ask your owner or administrator to **reset your password** for you in **Employees**. They receive a
temporary password to give you; you then choose your own at sign-in.
:::

## Too many attempts

After several wrong passwords in a row GPOS asks you to **wait a minute** before trying again. This
protects your business from people guessing passwords.

## Signing out

Select <span class="ui">Sign out</span> at the bottom of the menu. Always sign out on shared
computers.
