:::note[Depends on your plan]
This page is part of the **POS checkout, cash shifts and POS devices** feature. If it is not in your plan, you will not see it in the menu. See [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).
:::

| | **Void** | **Return** |
|-|----------|-----------|
| What | Cancels the **whole** sale | Gives back **some or all** items |
| When | During the **same shift** the sale was made in | Later, within the business's **return window** (30 days by default) |
| Refund | The way it was paid | To the method you choose |

Both need an **open shift** (refunds come out of its drawer) and, unless your role allows it, a
**manager's approval**.

## Find the sale

1. Select <span class="ui">Sales</span> (**F9**) to open **Sales on this till**. Here you can also
   reprint a receipt or <span class="ui">Print A4</span> a full-page invoice.
2. **Search receipt number**, or pick the sale from the list.
3. Sale from **another till**? Type the receipt number and select
   <span class="ui">Find … (another till)</span> — this needs internet.

## Return items

1. On the sale, select <span class="ui">Return</span> (then <span class="ui">Return items</span>).
2. For each line, enter the **Quantity to return** (or <span class="ui">All</span>). The screen
   shows what was sold and what was returned already.
3. Choose a **Reason** — *Faulty*, *Wrong item*, *Changed mind* or your own.
4. Tick **Back to stock (resellable)** if the item can be sold again. Leave it unticked for
   damaged goods — they are recorded as not restocked.
5. Choose **Refund to** — for example *Cash* — and for card, QR or bank refunds type the **Refund
   reference** from the terminal or app. A non-cash method can take back at most what was paid
   with it; refund the rest in cash.
6. Select <span class="ui">Refund {amount}</span> and get a manager's approval if asked.

A **RETURN / REFUND** receipt is printed with the original receipt number.

## Void a sale

1. On the sale, select <span class="ui">Void</span> (then <span class="ui">Void sale</span>).
2. Choose a **Reason**. The screen shows how it is **refunded the way it was paid**.
3. Select <span class="ui">Void the sale</span> and get approval if asked.

A **VOID** receipt is printed.

## Good to know

- Offline, the till only knows returns made **on this till**.
- *This sale is older than the business's return window* — the return window has passed.
- Returns and voids appear in **Sales** (*Returned*, *Partly returned*, *Voided*) and in the
  **Refunds & voids** report.
