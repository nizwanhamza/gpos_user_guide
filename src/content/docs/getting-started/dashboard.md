The **Dashboard** is the first page after you sign in.

## What you see

- **Today** — net sales, number of sales, average sale and returns so far today, with a link to
  <span class="ui">All reports</span>.
- **Best sellers today** — the products selling most today.
- **Business** — your business name, **Business code**, currency, time zone, contact and the date
  you joined.
- **Your access** — the roles and permissions you have, and for which branches.
- **Plan** — your plan's name, every feature your plan includes (marked ✓), and its
  limits: active branches, registers, employees, products, and — when they are in your plan —
  warehouses and customer profiles. *To change your plan, contact your
  service provider.* See [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).

What appears depends on your permissions. A cashier, for example, does not see sales figures.

The **menu** on the left shows your business name (with your [logo](/business/settings/#logo), if
you uploaded one). At the bottom it shows the **version** of GPOS you are using, for example
*Version 2026.10.29-1*. Tell your service provider this number when you report a problem.

## Working with lists

Every list in the back office — sales, shifts, products, customers, stock movements, purchase
orders, suppliers, transfers, employees, POS devices, the audit log and more — works the same way:

- **Search** box at the top (each list says what you can search, e.g. *Search receipt, customer
  or cashier*);
- **filters** such as branch, status or **From / To** dates;
- select a **column heading** to sort by it;
- the count shows how many are shown, e.g. *25 of 1,240*; select <span class="ui">Load more</span>
  for the next ones.

## The setup checklist

New businesses see a card called **Get your business ready** at the top of the dashboard. It shows
progress — for example *3 of 8 done* — and these steps:

| # | Step | Where it takes you | Ticks itself when… | Can be marked by hand |
|---|------|--------------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| 1 | Business details and receipt header | Settings | phone, address or receipt header lines are filled in | <span class="ui">Done</span> |
| 2 | Tax | Catalog setup → Taxes | an active tax rate exists | <span class="ui">We do not charge VAT</span> |
| 3 | Payment methods | Settings | a payment method was changed or added | <span class="ui">These are fine</span> |
| 4 | Products | Products | at least one product exists | — |
| 5 | Opening stock | Inventory → Opening stock | any stock has been posted | <span class="ui">Skip for now</span> |
| 6 | Pair the first till | POS devices | a till is registered | — |
| 7 | Add staff | Employees | there is more than one active employee | <span class="ui">I work alone</span> |
| 8 | Make the first sale | opens the till | the first sale has reached the back office | — |

Tips:

- Each step has an <span class="ui">Open</span> button that takes you to the right screen.
- On the **Tax** step, <span class="ui">Add VAT 18%</span> creates the standard VAT rate and a
  *VAT 18%* tax group in one click.
- Marked a step by mistake? Select <span class="ui">Not done</span> to undo it.
- Steps for features that are not in your plan are left out — so your checklist may have fewer
  than eight steps. See [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).
- Only people who can manage business settings (owners and administrators) see the checklist.

When everything is done the card says **Your business is ready**. Select
<span class="ui">Hide checklist</span> to remove it.

### Bring the checklist back

Hid it too early? Go to **Settings** and select **Show the setup checklist on the dashboard
again**.

The step-by-step version of the checklist is the [Quick start](/quick-start/).
