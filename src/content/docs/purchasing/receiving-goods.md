Goods received go into the store's stock **at their cost**, and the average cost is updated.

## Receive against a purchase order

1. Go to **Purchasing → Purchase orders**, open the order and select
   <span class="ui">Receive goods</span>.
2. GPOS fills in what was ordered. **Adjust quantities and costs to what arrived.**
3. Enter the **Supplier invoice no.** and **Invoice date**.
4. For products with batch tracking, enter the **Batch number** and **Expiry date**.
5. If nothing more will come, tick **Nothing more will come for this order (close it)**.
6. Select <span class="ui">Post goods received</span>.

GPOS shows *Received as GRN-…* — the goods are in stock.

## Receive without an order

1. Go to **Purchasing → Receive goods** → <span class="ui">Receive without an order</span>.
2. Choose the **Supplier** and **Store**.
3. Add items with **Unit** (or pack), **Quantity** and **Cost per unit**.
4. Enter the invoice details and select <span class="ui">Post goods received</span>.

## Packs and cases

If you buy in cases but sell in pieces, choose the pack as the **Unit** and enter the **pack size**
(*pieces per pack*) and the **cost per pack**. GPOS converts it to pieces in stock.

The **Purchases** report shows goods received less returns, by supplier, product or month.
