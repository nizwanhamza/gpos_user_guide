**Quick-pick buttons** are product buttons on the till's main screen — ideal for items **without
barcodes**, such as loose vegetables, bakery items or a cup of tea. Each **branch** has its own
set of up to **40** buttons.

## Set up the buttons

1. Go to **Branches** and select <span class="ui">Quick-pick buttons</span> on the branch.
2. **Search products** on the right and add them.
3. Use <span class="ui">Move up</span> / <span class="ui">Move down</span> to put the most used
   items first, and <span class="ui">Remove</span> to take one off.
4. Save.

Tills receive changes with their next sync.

:::tip
Combine quick picks with **Sold by: Weight** products: the cashier taps *Carrots*, puts them on the
scale, types the weight, and the price is worked out.
:::
