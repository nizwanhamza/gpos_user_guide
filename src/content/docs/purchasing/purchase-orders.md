:::note[Depends on your plan]
**Purchase orders** is a plan feature (it needs **Suppliers and goods received**). Without it, the **Purchase orders** tab does not appear — you can still receive goods without an order. See [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).
:::

A **purchase order (PO)** is what you order from a supplier. Using POs is optional — you can also
[receive goods without an order](/purchasing/receiving-goods/#receive-without-an-order).

## Create a purchase order

1. Go to **Purchasing → Purchase orders** → <span class="ui">New purchase order</span>.
2. Choose the **Supplier** and the **Store** the goods go to.
3. **Add an item**: search name or SKU. For each line set:
   - **Unit** — the product's unit, or a **pack** (for example a case of 24 — enter the **pack
     size**);
   - **Quantity** and **Cost per unit**.
4. Optionally enter **Expected on** and a **Note**.
5. Select <span class="ui">Save draft</span>.

## Send it

Open the order and select <span class="ui">Print / save as PDF</span> to send it to the supplier,
then <span class="ui">Mark as ordered</span>.

Only the order is printed, on A4 — no menus or buttons:

- your **letterhead** — logo (if you have one), business name, address, phone, e-mail and tax
  registration no.;
- **Purchase order** with its number, **Order date**, **Expected delivery** and **Status**;
- the **Supplier** (with their tax no. and payment terms) and where to **Deliver to**;
- the items with quantity, cost per unit and line totals, the **Total**, and your **Note**;
- at the bottom, *Printed … by …*.

To e-mail it, choose **Save as PDF** in the print window. The file name is suggested for you, for
example *Purchase order PO-000012*.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Being prepared; can still be edited. |
| **Ordered** | Sent to the supplier. |
| **Partly received** | Some goods arrived. |
| **Received** | Everything arrived, or the order was closed. |
| **Cancelled** | Not going ahead. |

## When goods arrive

Open the order and select <span class="ui">Receive goods</span> — see
[Receiving goods](/purchasing/receiving-goods/).

- Supplier will not send the rest? <span class="ui">Close (nothing more expected)</span>.
- Order not needed? <span class="ui">Cancel order</span>.

The **Open purchase orders** report lists goods still to arrive.
