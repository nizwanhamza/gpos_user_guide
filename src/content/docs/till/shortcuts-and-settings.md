## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **F2** | Go to the scan box |
| **F3** | Customer |
| **F4** | Pay |
| **F6** | Hold the sale |
| **F7** | Held sales |
| **F8** | Discount on the whole sale |
| **F9** | Sales on this till (returns, voids, reprints) |
| **F10** | Shift (cash in/out, X report, close) |

You can also just start typing or scanning — the till sends it to the scan box.

## Till settings

Open the sync panel (select the status bar) to find the till's own settings. They apply to **this
device** only.

### Receipt printing

- **Receipt paper** — *58 mm* or *80 mm* (80 mm by default).
- **Receipts** — tick **Print after each sale** to print automatically.

Receipts print through the browser to the printer installed on the device. Set the receipt printer
as the **default printer** and, in Chrome or Edge, turn off headers and footers in the print
dialog. For silent printing without a dialog, ask your IT support about Chrome's *kiosk printing*
option.

What is printed (name, header lines, footer, tax number) is set in
[Settings → Receipts](/business/settings/#receipts).

### Screen

- **Screen** (in the sync panel) — the till keeps the screen on while it is open, if the browser
  allows it.
- In the **top bar**: <span class="ui">Larger text</span> / <span class="ui">Normal text</span> for
  bigger buttons and prices, and <span class="ui">Full screen</span> /
  <span class="ui">Exit full screen</span>.

### Scan sounds

Turn the beep on scanning **On** or off.
