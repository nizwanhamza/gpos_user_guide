:::note[Depends on your plan]
This is the plan feature **Sell on Gocart.lk**. Without it, there is no **Sell online** in the
menu. If the page says *Selling on Gocart.lk is not set up on this system yet*, contact your
service provider. See [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).
:::

With **Sell online** you open a seller account on the **Gocart.lk** marketplace and sell chosen
items there. **Prices and stock follow GPOS**, so you never sell online what you do not have.
Gocart.lk reviews your shop and each product before it goes on sale. Orders are packed and shipped
in the **Gocart.lk Seller app**.

## Who can do what

| Permission | Who has it by default | Allows |
|------------|-----------------------|--------|
| **View online marketplace selling (connection and listings)** | Owner, Administrator, Manager | See the Sell online page, items and orders. |
| **Connect to an online marketplace and choose what is sold there** | Owner, Administrator | Apply, choose the store, list items, stop selling. |

You can give these to other roles in [Roles](/business/roles/).

## 1. Apply

1. Go to **Sell online**.
2. Check the details GPOS has filled in from your business — **Shop name**, **Business
   registration (BR) number** (optional), **Owner's first name** and **last name**, **E-mail**,
   **Mobile number** (digits with 94, e.g. `94771234567`), **Address**, **City**, **District** and
   **Postal code**.
3. Enter the **NIC number** of the owner or a director (9 digits and V or X, or 12 digits).
4. Optionally write **About the business** — it is shown on your shop page.
5. Select <span class="ui">Apply to Gocart.lk</span>.

Your details go to Gocart.lk for its seller review. **The NIC is not kept in GPOS.** Use an e-mail
you will keep: it becomes the sign-in of the seller account.

## 2. Wait for approval

The page shows **Waiting for approval** and *Applied …*. When Gocart.lk approves your shop:

- the owner gets an **e-mail with a link to choose the seller account's password** — this is the
  sign-in for the Gocart.lk Seller app, where you handle orders;
- GPOS notices the approval within a few minutes. Select <span class="ui">Check now</span> to look
  at once.

Not approved? The page says so — correct the details and apply again.

## 3. Choose where the stock comes from

When the shop is **Active**:

- **Stock from** — the store whose stock is offered on Gocart.lk. Only stock movements in this
  store count.
- **Keep back** — how many units of each item are **never** offered online, for example to keep a
  few for walk-in customers.

The top of the page shows your shop (<span class="ui">View the shop</span>), whether **Products
approved automatically** is on, and a count such as *12 items for sale online: 10 on sale, 1
waiting for approval, 1 not accepted*.

## 4. Match your categories

Under **Categories on Gocart.lk**, choose the Gocart.lk category for each of **Your categories**.
It is suggested when you list an item; you can still change it per item.

## 5. Choose what to sell

The **Items** list shows each item with its **Online price**, **In stock** and its state **On
Gocart.lk**. Search by name, SKU or barcode, and filter: *All items*, *For sale online*, *Not for
sale online*, *Not accepted*.

To sell an item, select <span class="ui">Sell online</span> on its row:

1. Tick **Sell this item on Gocart.lk**.
2. Check the **Category on Gocart.lk**.
3. **Online price (Rs)** — leave empty to use the selling price including tax, or enter a
   different price for online.
4. If asked, enter the **weight** (in kg, packed, e.g. `0.5`). Choose the **Delivery charge**: *As set on
   Gocart.lk*, *By weight*, *Post office cash on delivery*, *Free delivery* or *Special charge*
   (then enter the **Special delivery charge (Rs)**, charged per item).
5. Fill in any fields the Gocart.lk category asks for. Optional parts open when needed:
   **Special price**, **Package size**, **Descriptions** (leave empty to use the product's own
   description) and **Search engines (meta)**.
6. Save.

The **name, price and photos come from the product** in GPOS. Later changes to price, name, photos
and stock are **sent automatically**.

### Item states

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Not listed** | Not for sale online. |
| **Sending…** | Being sent to Gocart.lk. |
| **Waiting for approval** | Gocart.lk is reviewing the product. |
| **On sale** | Customers can buy it. |
| **On sale, changes waiting** | On sale; your latest changes are being sent. |
| **Taken off sale** | No longer for sale online. |
| **Not accepted** | Gocart.lk did not accept it. Open the item to see why (usually a field the category requires), fix it and save — it is sent again. Or select <span class="ui">Send again</span>. |

Good to know:

- Items **sold by weight** cannot be sold online yet (*Sold by weight: later*).
- Items whose stock is **not tracked** are offered as 100 in stock.
- An item with its own **online price** keeps it when you change the shop price. Prices you
  schedule for a later date go online with the item's next change.

## Online orders

Customers order on Gocart.lk, and you **pack and ship in the Gocart.lk Seller app**. Within about a
minute GPOS reads each order and takes the items out of the **Stock from** store:

- in **Inventory → Movements** they show as **Sold online** (document `#` + the order number);
- a cancelled order gives the stock back (**Online order cancelled**);
- a **refunded** order is recorded but **does not** add stock back — if the goods come back,
  [adjust the stock](/inventory/adjustments/) yourself.

**Orders on Gocart.lk**, at the bottom of Sell online, lists every order with **When**, **Order**,
**What happened** (*Ordered*, *Cancelled*, *Refunded*) and the **Stock** change.

Online orders are not till sales: they do not appear in **Sales** or in cash shifts. In the
**Stock movement summary** report, *sold* includes items sold online.

## Keeping both sides the same

Every night GPOS compares your items with Gocart.lk and sends again whatever differs. The page shows
*Compared with the marketplace …: … items checked, … sent again.* Select
<span class="ui">Compare now</span> to do it at once. A small difference while an order is being
read is normal.

## Stop selling

Select <span class="ui">Stop selling on Gocart.lk</span> and confirm with
<span class="ui">Stop selling</span>. Your products on Gocart.lk are **taken off sale (not
deleted)** and GPOS stops sending to it. Orders already placed are still handled in the Seller app.
If you apply again later, your same seller account is reconnected.
