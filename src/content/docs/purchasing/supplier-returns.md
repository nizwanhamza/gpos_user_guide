---
title: Returns to suppliers
description: Send faulty or unwanted goods back to the supplier.
sidebar:
  order: 4
---

:::note[Depends on your plan]
**Supplier returns** is a plan feature (it needs **Suppliers and goods received**). Without it, the **Returns** tab does not appear. See [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).
:::

Goods sent back **leave the store's stock at the average cost**.

1. Go to **Purchasing → Returns to suppliers** → <span class="ui">Return goods</span>.
2. Choose the **Supplier** and **Store**.
3. Add the items and quantities.
4. Enter a **Reason** (for example *Damaged in delivery*).
5. Select <span class="ui">Post return</span>.

The return appears in the list with its **Value at cost**, and in the stock ledger as *Returned to
supplier*.
