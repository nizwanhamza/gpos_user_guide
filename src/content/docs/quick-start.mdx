Welcome to GPOS! Your business has been enrolled and you have received:

- your **business code** (a short name for your business, for example `kandy-mart`),
- the **e-mail and first password** of the owner account.

<Aside type="note" title="GPOS addresses">
- **Back office** (owners, managers, office staff): **[https://app.gpos.lk](https://app.gpos.lk)**
- **Till / POS** (the checkout counter): **[https://app.gpos.lk/pos/](https://app.gpos.lk/pos/)**

Bookmark both. Always type them with `https://`.
</Aside>

This page takes you from there to your first sale. Plan about **an hour** — less if you import your
products from a spreadsheet.

<Aside type="tip" title="Follow the checklist on your dashboard">
After you sign in, the dashboard shows a card called **Get your business ready** with the same
eight steps as this page. Each step has an <span class="ui">Open</span> button that takes you
straight to the right screen, and it **ticks itself** when GPOS sees the step is done.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Features depend on your plan">
GPOS comes in different plans — for example **Basic**, **Standard** or **Custom**. Some features in
this guide (such as inventory, purchasing, customers, several branches or expiry tracking) may not
be in your plan, and your checklist may have fewer than eight steps. Check the **Plan** card on
your dashboard and see [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).
</Aside>

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- A computer with **Chrome or Edge** for the back office.
- The **till device** — a counter computer, laptop or tablet with Chrome or Edge. A USB or
  Bluetooth barcode scanner and a receipt printer (58 mm or 80 mm) are optional but recommended.
- Your **product list** with prices (and barcodes if you have them). A spreadsheet is ideal.
- Your **VAT registration** details, if you are VAT-registered.
- The **names and e-mail addresses** of the staff who will use GPOS.

## Step 0 — Sign in for the first time

<Steps>

1. Open the back office at **[app.gpos.lk](https://app.gpos.lk)**.
2. Enter your **Business code**, **Email** and **Password**, then select <span class="ui">Sign in</span>.
3. You are asked to **choose a new password** (at least 12 characters — a memorable phrase works
   well). Do this now; the first password was only temporary.
4. Read and accept the **Terms of service** (and the privacy and data-processing terms): tick
   the box and select <span class="ui">Accept and continue</span>. Only the owner is asked.
5. If GPOS asks you to **Set up two-step sign-in**, scan the QR code with an authenticator app on
   your phone and keep the recovery codes safe — see
   [Two-step sign-in](/getting-started/two-step-sign-in/). Even when it is optional, we recommend
   it for the owner.
6. Optional: pick your language with the language switch (English, සිංහල or தமிழ்).

</Steps>

More detail: [Signing in](/getting-started/signing-in/).

## Step 1 — Business details and receipt header

Tell GPOS who you are. This information is printed on your receipts.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Settings** → **Business profile**.
2. Fill in your **Phone**, **Contact email** and the **Address** section.
3. Check **Regional settings**: currency, time zone and the **Default language** for your staff.
4. Scroll to **Receipts** and add the **Header lines** printed at the top of each receipt (up to 4
   lines — for example your address and hotline) and a **Footer text** such as *Thank you, come
   again!*
5. Select <span class="ui">Save settings</span>.

</Steps>

Deep dive: [Business settings](/business/settings/).

## Step 2 — Tax

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="We are VAT-registered">

On the checklist, select <span class="ui">Add VAT 18%</span>. GPOS creates a **VAT** tax rate of 18%
and a tax group **VAT 18%** for you. Then give your taxable products the tax group *VAT 18%* (you can
do this while adding products in Step 4, or in the import spreadsheet's `tax_group` column with the
code `VAT18`).

By default **prices include tax** — the shelf price is what the customer pays and the tax is worked
out of it. To add tax on top at checkout instead, untick **Prices include tax** in
**Settings → Products and prices**.

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="We do not charge VAT">

Select <span class="ui">We do not charge VAT</span> on the checklist. Products without a tax group
are not taxed. You can add tax rates later at any time.

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

<Aside type="caution">
Confirm your tax rates with your accountant before you start selling.
</Aside>

Deep dive: [Taxes](/catalog/taxes/).

## Step 3 — Payment methods

**Cash, Card, QR payment and Bank transfer** are ready to use. Card, QR and bank payments are taken
on your bank's terminal or app — GPOS only records them with the reference your cashier types.

- If you accept all of them, select <span class="ui">These are fine</span>.
- Otherwise go to **Settings → Payment methods** and **switch off** what you do not take, or
  <span class="ui">Add payment method</span> for your own (for example a gift voucher).

Deep dive: [Payment methods](/business/payment-methods/).

## Step 4 — Products

Choose the way that suits you:

<Tabs>
<TabItem label="Import a spreadsheet (many products)">

<Steps>

1. Go to **Products** → <span class="ui">Import / export</span>.
2. Download the **Excel template** (or CSV template). Fill in **one row per item** — name, unit
   (`pcs`, `kg`…), SKU, barcodes, cost and selling price.
3. Select <span class="ui">Upload file…</span>. Nothing changes yet — GPOS shows what it would
   create and lists any problems by row.
4. Fix any problems in the file and upload it again. When it says **Ready to import**, select
   <span class="ui">Import now</span>.

</Steps>

Deep dive: [Import and export](/catalog/import-export/).

</TabItem>
<TabItem label="Add products one by one">

<Steps>

1. Go to **Products** → <span class="ui">New product</span>.
2. Enter the **Name**, choose the **Unit** and the **Tax group**.
3. Under **Codes and price**, enter the **SKU**, **Barcodes** and **Selling price**.
4. Select <span class="ui">Create product</span>.

</Steps>

Deep dive: [Products, variants and prices](/catalog/products/).

</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Step 5 — Opening stock

So that stock levels start right, enter what is on your shelves today.

<Steps>

1. Go to **Inventory** → **Opening stock** and choose the **Store**.
2. Paste rows from a spreadsheet — **SKU or barcode, quantity, optional unit cost** — or upload a CSV.
3. Select <span class="ui">Check</span>. When all rows are fine, select <span class="ui">Post opening stock</span>.

</Steps>

Not tracking stock yet? Select <span class="ui">Skip for now</span> on the checklist.

Deep dive: [Opening stock](/inventory/opening-stock/).

## Step 6 — Pair the first till

<Steps>

1. Add a **register** (a checkout counter) first: go to **Branches**, and in your branch (called
   **MAIN** to start with) select <span class="ui">Add</span> under **Registers**. Give it a code and
   name, for example `R1` — *Counter 1*.
2. Go to **POS devices**. Next to the register, select <span class="ui">Set up a till</span>.
   A **one-time code** and a QR code appear (valid for 15 minutes).
3. On the till device, open **[app.gpos.lk/pos](https://app.gpos.lk/pos/)** in Chrome or Edge.
4. Enter the **Setup code** (or scan the QR code), give the till a name such as *Front counter*, and
   select <span class="ui">Register this till</span>.
5. Wait while the till downloads the catalog. From now on it also works **offline**.

</Steps>

Deep dive: [Set up a till](/till/set-up-a-till/).

## Step 7 — Add staff

<Steps>

1. Go to **Employees** → <span class="ui">Add employee</span>.
2. Enter their **Full name**, **Email** and an **Initial password** (at least 12 characters).
3. Give them a **Role** — for example *Cashier* — and the **Branch** they work in.
4. Select <span class="ui">Create employee</span>.
5. To let them unlock the till, open the employee and select <span class="ui">Set POS PIN</span>
   (6 digits). Tell them the PIN in person. They can also set it themselves under **My account**.

</Steps>

Working alone? Select <span class="ui">I work alone</span> on the checklist — but **set your own
POS PIN** in **My account**, because you need it to unlock the till.

Deep dive: [Employees](/business/employees/) · [Roles and permissions](/business/roles/).

## Step 8 — Make the first sale

<Steps>

1. On the till, choose your name and **enter your PIN**.
2. **Open a shift**: count the cash in the drawer (the *opening float*) and select <span class="ui">Open shift</span>.
3. **Scan a barcode** or type a product name. The item appears in the sale.
4. Select <span class="ui">Pay</span> (or press **F4**), enter the **Cash received** and select
   <span class="ui">Complete sale</span>. The till shows the change to give.

</Steps>

🎉 The sale now appears in **Sales** and **Reports** in the back office, and the checklist shows
**Your business is ready**. You can select <span class="ui">Hide checklist</span>.

Deep dive: [Making a sale](/till/making-a-sale/) · [Cash shifts on the till](/till/shifts/).

<Aside type="caution" title="About discounts and cash outs">
Out of the box, **every** discount a cashier gives and **every** cash out needs a manager's PIN. To
relax this, see [Checkout and cash](/business/settings/#checkout-and-cash).
</Aside>
