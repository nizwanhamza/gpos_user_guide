:::note[Depends on your plan]
This page is part of the **Selling at the counter (POS), cash shifts and tills** feature. Doing cash in and cash out on the till is the separate plan feature **Cash in / cash out**. See [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).
:::

When a cashier puts cash **into** the drawer (for example extra change) or takes it **out** (for
example to pay a delivery), they choose a **reason**. Manage reasons in **Settings → Cash in/out
reasons**. Tills use the active reasons, also offline.

## Ready-made reasons

| Name | For | Note required |
|------|-----|---------------|
| Float top-up | Cash in | No |
| Paid out | Cash out | No |
| Bank deposit | Cash out | No |
| To the safe | Cash out | No |
| Other | Cash in and out | Yes |

Edit, reorder or switch them off to suit your shop.

:::caution
If no active reason exists for cash in or for cash out, cashiers cannot do it — the till shows
*No reasons are set up for this*.
:::

## Add a reason

1. Select <span class="ui">Add reason</span>.
2. Enter the **Name** (and optionally the Sinhala and Tamil names).
3. Choose **For**: *Cash in*, *Cash out* or *Cash in and out*.
4. Tick **The cashier must write a note** if you want an explanation every time.
5. Set the **Order** and save.

Ideas for your own: *Supplier payment* (cash out, note required), *Staff advance* (cash out, note
required).

## Switch off or delete

A reason that was used can only be switched off; past movements keep the name they had. Unused
reasons can be deleted.

Cash outs above your limit need a manager's PIN — see
[Cash out without a manager up to](/business/settings/#cash-out-without-a-manager-up-to).
