GPOS is a **yearly subscription**. The **Billing** page in the back office shows your plan, the
dates you are paid for, what you owe, your invoices and how to pay.

You can subscribe **directly with GPOS**, or **through a GPOS partner** — a company that sells
GPOS to its own customers and looks after their billing. Everything in the back office and on the
tills works the same either way; only who bills you is different (see
[Subscribed through a partner?](#subscribed-through-a-partner)).

## Who can see it

| Permission | Who by default | Allows |
|------------|----------------|--------|
| **See the plan, subscription dates and billing** | Owner, Administrator | Open **Billing** in the menu. |
| **Change the plan, cancel the subscription and use promotion codes** | Owner, Administrator | The plan, promotion and cancellation buttons on Billing. |

## Your subscription

Go to **Billing**. The **Subscription** card shows:

- your **Plan**;
- the **Paid period**, **Valid until** (or **Free trial until**) and **Days left** — or *… days
  overdue*;
- the **Yearly price** — or *Complimentary*, for accounts arranged with GPOS;
- **Billed by** — GPOS or your partner;
- a status:

| Status | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Free trial** | You use the full plan free until the trial ends. |
| **Active** | Paid up. You are reminded by e-mail **30, 7 and 1 days** before the year ends. |
| **Payment due** | The paid year has ended. Everything keeps working for now — please renew. |
| **Payment overdue: read-only** | Still unpaid: changes in the back office are paused (see [below](#when-a-payment-is-late)). |
| **Suspended** | The account is suspended for non-payment. Contact GPOS to pay. |
| **Cancelled** | You cancelled. Everything works until the last day of the year, then it ends. |
| **Ended** | The subscription has ended. Contact GPOS to start again. |

Below it, the page also shows your plan's features and how much of each limit you use — the same as
the **Plan** card on the [dashboard](/getting-started/dashboard/).

:::note[No end date?]
Some accounts — for example businesses that joined before billing started — have **No end date**
and are *Complimentary*. Their plan is arranged with GPOS: contact GPOS to change it.
:::

## Invoices and paying

About **7 days before** your year (or free trial) ends, GPOS issues the **invoice for the next
year**, due on the last day. You receive it by e-mail.

- When something is left to pay, Billing shows **To pay: …** with the **bank details**. **Quote the
  invoice number** (for example `INV-2026-000123`) with your payment.
- **Invoices** lists each invoice with **Issued**, **Due**, **Total** and **Left to pay**. Select
  <span class="ui">Print / PDF</span> to print it or save it as a PDF.
- When GPOS records your payment you get a confirmation e-mail, and the invoice shows **Paid**. Once
  the next year's invoice is paid in full, your subscription moves on to the new year.

A correction to an invoice is made with a **credit note**, shown on the invoice.

## Change your plan

Under **Plans**, compare the plans with their yearly prices and select
<span class="ui">Choose this plan</span>:

| When | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| **During the free trial** | The new plan starts at once; your trial continues. |
| **Upgrade** (a dearer plan) | You get an invoice for the **days left** in your year. The new plan starts as soon as that invoice is paid. |
| **Downgrade** (a cheaper plan) | The new plan starts when your **next year** begins; nothing changes until then. Changed your mind? Select <span class="ui">Keep my current plan</span> before the renewal invoice is issued. |

If you already have more than the new plan allows (for example more branches), GPOS warns you
first. **Nothing is removed** — you keep what you have but cannot add more. There is no refund for
a downgrade during the year.

When the new plan applies, its features appear the next time you open or reload the back office.
See [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/).

## Promotion codes

Got a **promotion code**? Under **Promotions and cancellation**, enter it in **Promotion code** and
select <span class="ui">Apply</span>. The page then shows the promotion — for example a discount on
your next renewal, on several renewals or on every renewal, or extra free months. One promotion
runs at a time. You can also enter a code when you [apply for GPOS](/getting-started/apply/).

## Cancel the subscription

1. Under **Promotions and cancellation**, select <span class="ui">Cancel subscription</span>.
2. Tell us **Why are you leaving?** (it helps us improve).
3. Select <span class="ui">Cancel at the end of the year</span>.

Everything keeps working until the last day of your year; then the subscription ends. Until then
you can select <span class="ui">Take back the cancellation</span>. Export anything you want to keep
(for example [products](/catalog/import-export/) and [reports](/reports/reports/)) before it ends.

## Reminders and banners

- **E-mails** to the owner: the trial ending (7 and 1 days before), renewal due (30, 7 and 1 days
  before), invoice issued, payment received, the year ended unpaid, plan changes and price changes.
- **A banner at the top of the back office** (for those who see Billing) when the trial or the paid
  year ends within 30 days, a payment is due, or the subscription is cancelled. Select
  <span class="ui">Open Billing</span>.

## When a payment is late

The shop is never stopped from serving customers because of billing. If the year ends without
payment, depending on your service provider's settings:

1. **Payment due** — everything keeps working for a few days (7 by default), with reminders.
2. **Payment overdue: read-only** — the back office becomes **read-only**: you can still see
   everything, run reports and export, and use **Billing** and **My account**, but changes are
   refused with *The subscription is unpaid: changes are paused until it is renewed*. **The tills
   keep selling and syncing.**
3. **Suspended** — after a few more days (7 by default), the account is suspended: nobody can sign
   in and the tills cannot sync (they keep sales made offline and send them once it is active
   again).

**As soon as your payment is recorded, everything works again.** While payment is due or the back
office is read-only, the tills show a line for the cashier to pass on, for example *The business's
subscription is waiting for payment. Please tell the owner. Selling continues as usual.*

## Subscribed through a partner?

If you subscribed through a GPOS partner, Billing says *Your plan and payments are handled by your
partner …* with the partner's phone and e-mail.

- The partner bills you **in their own way** — GPOS does not send you invoices.
- To **change plan or renew**, contact your partner; the plans and promotion codes are not shown.
  You can still [cancel](#cancel-the-subscription) at the end of the year — tell your partner too.
- Your partner sees your **plan and subscription dates only** — never your sales, products,
  customers or other business data.

You can sign up through a partner using the registration link they give you — see
[Apply for GPOS](/getting-started/apply/).
