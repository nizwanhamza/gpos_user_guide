Not using GPOS yet? Apply online — the form takes only a few minutes. A person at GPOS reviews every application and e-mails you,
usually **within a few working days**. Your business account is only created once your
application is approved.

**Apply at: [https://app.gpos.lk/register](https://app.gpos.lk/register)** — or select
**New business? Apply for an account** on the [sign-in page](https://app.gpos.lk).

:::tip[Buying GPOS through a partner?]
GPOS is also sold by **partners** — companies that set it up and bill you themselves. Use the
registration link your partner gives you: the form then says *You are signing up through our
partner …*. Your partner may also send the application for you. See
[Billing and subscription](/getting-started/billing/#subscribed-through-a-partner).
:::

:::note
If the page says *We are not taking new applications online at the moment*, contact GPOS Support
and they will set up your business for you.
:::

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- your **business name** and what kind of business it is;
- **your own e-mail address** — you will sign in with it once approved, so use one you will keep.
  We send a code to it while you apply, so keep your mailbox open;
- your mobile number.

That is all. If we need more — for example your business registration (BR) number, legal name
or address — we ask you after you apply.

## 1. Fill in the application

### Your business

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Business name** | The name customers know you by. |
| **Type of business** | For example grocery, pharmacy, clothing, restaurant. |

### About you

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| **Your full name** | |
| **Your role in the business** | Owner, director, partner, manager, accountant, employee or other. You do not have to be the owner, but you must be authorised to apply. |
| **Mobile number** | |
| **Language for e-mails** | English, Sinhala or Tamil. |
| **Your e-mail** | You sign in with this once approved. We e-mail a 6-digit code to it to confirm it. |

### Plan and promotion

- **Plan** — choose the plan you want, with its yearly price and free-trial days (if any), or
  *Not sure yet*. We confirm the plan when we approve. See [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/)
  and [Billing and subscription](/getting-started/billing/).
- **Promotion code** (optional) — if you have one. It is applied when your application is approved.

Then:

1. Tick **I am authorised to apply for this business, and the details are correct**.
2. Tick that you accept the terms (the links open them).
3. Complete the **robot check** if one is shown.
4. Select <span class="ui">Send application</span>.

## 2. Confirm your e-mail

GPOS shows **Check your e-mail** and asks for the **Code from the e-mail**.

1. Open the e-mail we sent you (subject *Your GPOS confirmation code: …*). Check your spam folder
   if it does not arrive.
2. Type the **6-digit code** on the page **within 30 minutes** and select
   <span class="ui">Confirm</span>.
3. Your application page opens. Your application is reviewed only after this step.

No e-mail, or the code expired? Select <span class="ui">Send a new code</span> — it can be used
again after a minute (the button counts down). The new code replaces the old one. After **5 wrong
tries** a code stops working: ask for a new one.

:::caution
Applications that are never confirmed are deleted after 7 days. You can have **one open
application per e-mail address** at a time: if you fill in the form again with the same e-mail,
we e-mail you a code that opens the application you already sent.
:::

## 3. Follow your application

Select **Check my application** on the sign-in page (or **I already applied** on the form), enter
the e-mail you applied with and select <span class="ui">Send me a code</span>. Type the 6-digit
code from the e-mail (valid 30 minutes) and select <span class="ui">Confirm</span>: **Your
application** opens, with its status and history.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **E-mail not confirmed** | Type the code we e-mailed you to confirm your address. |
| **Waiting for review** | A person is reviewing your application. We will e-mail you. |
| **More information needed** | We asked a question — see below. |
| **Approved** | Your business is ready. We e-mailed you a link to choose your password. |
| **Not approved** | The reason is in the history. |
| **Withdrawn** | You withdrew the application. |

### When we need more information

We e-mail you our question with a link (valid 7 days). On your application page:

1. Write **Your answer**.
2. Tick **Also correct my details** if something in the application was wrong, and correct it.
3. Select <span class="ui">Send answer</span>. Your application goes back for review.

We may ask, for example, for your legal form, business registration number, legal name or
address. Businesses in regulated trades — for example **pharmacies, clinics, hospitals or medical
supplies** — may be asked for their licence details.

### Changed your mind?

Select <span class="ui">Withdraw application</span>. You can apply again later.

## 4. Approved — activate your account

When your application is approved:

- your business is created with a main branch, and **you become its owner**;
- GPOS chooses your **business code** — the short code everyone in your business uses to sign
  in, for example `lanka-mart`. **It cannot be changed later**;
- the plan agreed for online applications is assigned (see [Plans and features](/getting-started/plans/));
- we e-mail you a link to **choose your password** — valid **7 days** — and your application page
  shows **Your business code**.

Then:

1. Open the link in the e-mail and choose a password (at least 12 characters).
2. Sign in at [app.gpos.lk](https://app.gpos.lk) with your **business code**, **e-mail** and new
   password.
3. Accept the terms of service and, if asked, set up [two-step sign-in](/getting-started/two-step-sign-in/).
4. Complete your business details — legal name, registration and tax numbers, phone, contact
   e-mail and address — in [Settings](/business/settings/). Until then the phone and e-mail are
   your own.
5. Continue with the **[Quick start](/quick-start/)**.

:::tip[Link expired?]
If you did not open the e-mail link within 7 days, contact GPOS Support and ask for a new link to
choose your password.
:::
