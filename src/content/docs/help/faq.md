### Does the till work without internet?

Yes. Selling, payments, discounts, approvals, shifts, cash in/out, returns from the same till and
adding customers all work offline. Sales are sent automatically when the connection is back. You
need internet to set up a till and to receive price changes. See [Offline and sync](/till/offline-sync/).

### What devices can I use as a till?

Any computer, laptop or tablet that runs **Chrome or Edge**. A barcode scanner, receipt printer
(58 or 80 mm) and cash drawer are optional.

### Can I use several tills in one shop?

Yes. Add a **register** for each counter in **Branches** and pair one till to each. Your plan
decides how many registers you can have.

### Does GPOS take card payments?

No — use your bank's card terminal or QR app as usual. GPOS records the payment with its
reference so your reports match the bank. See [Payments](/till/payments/).

### Why does every cashier discount ask for a manager?

That is the safe starting setting. Raise the **Discount limit for everyone else** in
[Settings](/business/settings/#discount-limits) — for example to 5 %.

### Can a cashier also use the back office?

Yes, with their e-mail and password. They only see what their role allows.

### I made a mistake in a sale. Can I edit it?

No — sales are never edited, so your records stay trustworthy. **Void** it in the same shift, or
**return** the items, then ring it up again. See [Returns and voids](/till/returns-voids/).

### How do I change a price for one branch only?

Open the product's **Prices** and choose *{branch} branch only*. See
[Prices](/catalog/products/#prices).

### Can I schedule a price change?

Yes. Choose *A later date and time* when you set the price. Tax rate changes can be scheduled too.

### How do I give receipts in Sinhala or Tamil?

Turn on **Use Sinhala/Tamil product names** in Settings and fill in the names. See
[Languages](/getting-started/languages/).

### How do I get my data out?

Export the catalog from **Products → Import / export**, and any report with
<span class="ui">Export CSV</span>.

### How do I change my plan?

Contact your service provider. Your current plan and limits are on the dashboard.

### Is my data visible to other businesses?

No. Each business only sees its own data. If your service provider helps you in a support session,
a banner is shown and everything is recorded in your [audit log](/reports/audit-log/).
